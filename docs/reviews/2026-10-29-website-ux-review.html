Maslow AI · Website review · October 29, 2026

www.maslow.ai: design, copy, ICP perception and motion story

The craft is excellent: the visual identity, the dimensional foundation story and the candor about product status. The site sells the wrong thing first. It opens with a free Linux OS that is in development and has no download, while the paid services that drive revenue sit behind about six screens of scroll. Neither half of the ICP (a business leader and their IT counterpart) is likely to book a call from the homepage.

Reviewed revision: main at 825258f (deployed site, captured October 29, 2026). Status: findings only, no site changes made. Open decision below must be resolved before implementation.

What is working

  • Visual identity. Premium and editorial, consistent with the Guide archetype. The 3D scenes render cleanly; the phone close-ups are well judged. LIVE
  • The hero handoff idea is the best concept on the site: switching Codex, Claude Code and Hermes rotates the agent card while the "Supplier review" context visibly stays put. LIVE
  • Trust content that reduces commitment risk: go/no-go gates at weeks 2, 4 and 10, "you keep the map," fixed fees quoted before work, explicit ownership and exit, the founder's CIO/CTO background, case studies that state their own limits, the no-email /plan-workflow planner, and the FAQ answer to "Do we have to replace Windows?" COPY
  • Motion engineering fundamentals: native scroll with a sticky pin (no scroll-jacking), a working Pause motion control, reduced-motion support, offscreen and context-loss handling, and chapter poses that settle before arrival. CODE
  • Best voice models on the site: /services and /how-we-engage (lowest hedge and jargon density, benefit-led). COPY

Findings

CriticalThe hero sells the free, unavailable product; the paid offer is a footnote

MASLOW AI-OS / FREE LINUX OS · IN DEVELOPMENT · "Your agents. One shared foundation." · "Maslow also builds paid AI workflows around your existing systems."

Five-second test for the business leader: "a Linux thing for AI agents, not finished, not for me." The first booking action ("Shape a workflow with us") appears after the 7 foundation chapters and the workspace section. Root cause is a conflict inside AGENTS.md: line 5 says "Lead with Maslow AI-OS" while line 6 says "Lead with a buyer's workflow, constraint, deliverable, or measured result," and line 39 requires a working-session action before deep reference content. The hero follows line 5. COPY · LIVE

CriticalProcurement gaps stop regulated buyers at IT review

/diligence: 6 of 7 artifacts "IN PREPARATION," including the healthcare annex (PHI posture, BAA availability) and the certificate of insurance. /security: "We do not currently hold SOC 2 certification."

Accurate and required by policy, but undated. For a healthcare IT lead this is a hard stop; for a manufacturer it signals "fine for a contained pilot only." COPY

Critical"Infinite AI OS" (client system) collides with "Maslow AI-OS" (product)

The site must repeatedly disclaim the difference ("These are Maslow client engagements, not deployments of the Linux AI-OS"). Proof and product cancel each other. COPY

HighThe secondary hero CTA leads to a dead end for the leader

"Explore the AI-OS preview" goes to /ai-os, which states there is no public download and asks for "a technical champion" and "a dedicated x86_64 test machine." Correct for the IT evaluator; an exit for the business leader. COPY

HighHedge density reads as "not ready"

Home has about 15 distinct disclaimer statements (25 hedge-term hits): "illustrative" 6, "evolving" 4, "configured for each organization" three times nearly verbatim. Per 100 words: resources 3.0, ai-os 2.5, home 1.7, versus services 0.3 and how-we-engage 0.2. The guardrails are accurate individually; together the page apologizes before it persuades. COPY

HighProof is buried and technical

OpenAI Select Partner appears only on the press page. Founder credentials appear only on /about. Home carries zero outcome numbers although real ones exist (4 AI employees live in Teams in 90 days; go/no-go gates). Case-study outcomes read "MEASUREMENT IN PROGRESS" and "93% expected first-tool routing," which do not translate for an executive. No testimonial, no named comparison with Copilot rollouts, Big-4 programs or systems integrators. COPY

HighCTA labels are fragmented

Booking has 7 labels ("Talk through a workflow," "LET'S TALK," "Map a workflow," "Map a workflow first," "Shape a workflow with us," "Build a workflow with us," "Talk through this starting point"). The preview has 6. Home body carries about 21 links against 2 late booking actions. The press release, the most shareable high-intent page, has no booking action. COPY · CODE

MediumJargon precedes benefit; brand positioning has drifted

Home jargon load is 3.5 per 100 words (Codex 9, Linux 6, SharePoint 7, plus Omarchy, Qdrant, Semantica). "Linux" appears at 2% depth and agent names at 4%, before any business problem. The brand tagline "Transform with purpose" and the brand value proposition appear on 0 of 25 routes; the footer runs three different taglines. About 18 headlines share the "Your X. Already here." two-beat pattern and "useful" appears in 7 headlines or CTAs. COPY

MediumNo price anchor anywhere

"Fixed fees are quoted before work begins" is reassuring but leaves the leader's first question open. COPY

LowMechanical issues

"Codex reviews the evidence ." (space before period), "for your way" (double space), mixed straight and curly apostrophes, nav "How We Help" vs footer "Services," shortened footers on /plan-workflow, /contact and /case-studies that drop the Trust links at decision time. COPY

Motion and story: is it landing?

Verdict: partially. The opening (files gather) and the close (layers reassemble) land on their own. The middle spends about 3.5 viewports on one repeated "a layer lifts out, a prop pops in" motion for three different ideas, and the claims that close deals (you keep control; switch agents without losing context) are told in copy rather than shown in motion.

Observed and measured

  • Scroll cost: the section is 690svh (650 on mobile), about 5.9 viewports pinned. Chapters 1 to 3 take 47% of it; Skills, Connections and Visibility get about 0.84 viewport each. CODE · LIVE
  • Legibility: in-scene labels are 6 to 7px; engraved tile text ("ACCESS," "KNOWLEDGE + MEMORY") is illegible at 1440px. Phone close-ups are readable. LIVE · CODE
  • Three numbering systems at once: "04 / 07," the "02 Skills" bottom nav, and the Files / Knowledge / Second brain sub-selector. LIVE
  • Performance: the Three.js scene is loaded with the page (dynamic(..., {ssr:false}) with no viewport gate, components/ai-os/FoundationJourney.tsx), contradicting docs/ai-os-buyer-journey.md. While visible it renders every frame: camera fit walks the scene each frame, line geometry is rebuilt, PCFSoftShadowMap at 1024 with DPR up to 1.8 (components/ai-os/scene-engine.js). Concept pages already gate correctly (components/stories/StorySculpture.tsx). CODE
  • Accessibility: pause buttons change label and toggle aria-pressed together ("Play animation, pressed"); aria-live announces while scrolling; reduced-motion users are still pinned for about 6 viewports; the no-WebGL fallback is four generic labels rather than chapter states. CODE
  • To verify on a device: during fast keyboard scrolling a white band briefly appeared with the sticky header displaced; it resolved once scrolling settled and may be a capture artifact. LIVE (unconfirmed)

Prioritized fixes

  1. Rewrite the hero around the paid outcome with one primary action, "Book a workflow review," a proof strip, and the OS as an explicit IT path. Move the handoff demo below the fold as the differentiator ("never locked to one AI vendor").
  2. One booking label site-wide. /plan-workflow becomes the optional pre-step ending in "Book a review of this brief." Add the booking action to the press release and every case-study ending. Retire "Let's talk," "Shape," "Build" and "Discuss."
  3. Consolidate hedges: one status line under the hero plus inline ILLUSTRATIVE tags beside claims; delete restating sentences and move cost and download terms to one footnote linked to /faq. Claim accuracy is preserved.
  4. Rename the manufacturing case study by outcome and lead case studies with business results and dated measurement targets. Keep "AI-OS" for the product only.
  5. Turn trust gaps into a dated plan on /security and /diligence; publish an interim one-page security summary and link it from /contact.
  6. Motion: auto-play the handoff once on first view (skip under reduced motion) with a visible "STAYS" beat; rebuild Visibility as a real backward trace ending at a person approving; show a person approving in chapter 3; cut the story to about 4 viewports and one numbering model; crossfade chapter copy in sync with the sculpture.
  7. Motion performance and access: viewport-gate the Three.js import; render only during transitions or pointer movement; compute camera fit once per pose; cap DPR at about 1.5 and use cheaper shadows on small screens; raise scene labels to at least 10px; fix pause-button ARIA; drop the sticky pin and show static chapter frames under reduced motion; make the no-WebGL fallback chapter-aware.
  8. Add a "Why Maslow vs. a Copilot rollout or a Big-4 program" section and a starting-price anchor on /how-we-engage.

Open decision

Is the homepage's job AI-OS adoption or booked services engagements? Every lens in this review recommends services first, with AI-OS as the IT path and differentiator. Implementing fixes 1 to 4 requires changing AGENTS.md line 5 ("Lead with Maslow AI-OS") so that line 6 ("Lead with a buyer's workflow...") takes precedence on the homepage. This review does not change that rule; the owner decides.
